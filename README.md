## **📖 About**

Tired of complex GUIs? Is inventory distribution a pain for you? Simple Sort is all about solving those troubles for you. It is simply a distribution platform that will transport items from your inventory into chests that have similar items. 

I built this mod for my server and friends, but discovered that there might be a need for it. Some people don't like complexity, and want to use simplified methods. 

This is my first mod, I hope you enjoy. If you discover any bugs, let me know! I've made sure to test as much as possible and didn't come across any game breaking issues.

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_c4696970-b63a-4031-9598-f95a3e0cb8f0.png)

## **ℹ️ How?**

Simple! Craft the Sort Platform (View gallery for recipe), place it down near other containers. Put in at-least 1 item, step on top of it, and watch your items transfer from your inventory to the chests. 

Want a chest to be a specific item? Place a glowing item frame on it, attach an item, and that container will be strictly filtered.

Default radius is 10x10x10 blocks. 

*   (Right Click) to view the radius
*   (Right Click & Shift) to lock the platform
*   (Step On) to transfer items
